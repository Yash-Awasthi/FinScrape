THE THESIS

Turn global events into auditable market calls.

01

Ingest

Geopolitics + world news across wire, gov, and intel tiers, plus keyless data APIs — GDELT conflict events and USGS earthquakes.

02

Resolve

Which companies, tickers, and sectors the event moves — role-tagged primary / competitor / supplier / regulator.

03

Judge

First- and second-order impact, a verdict (INVEST / PULL_OUT / OBSERVE / CAUTIOUS) and a −5…+5 score.

04

Prove

Every call scored against the realized price move — hit-rate, by-verdict, and an equity curve you can audit.

WHY IT'S TRUSTWORTHY

Every verdict has a chain of custody.

Calibrated

Scores are −5…+5 with explicit thresholds; ±5 reserved for genuinely extraordinary events. AI and heuristics cross-check.

Explainable

Every call carries its reasoning, affected companies and second-order effects; an on-demand multi-agent council adds consensus and dissent — never a black box.

Open proof

Hit-rate is backtested against realized moves and shown openly, with an equity curve. Open-source core (MIT).
